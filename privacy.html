Powder Peak – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Powder Peak is a snowboarding game made by Parker Murphy. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Powder Peak does not collect, send or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, unlocked maps, gear, mission stars, best scores, ghost race runs, custom stages and settings) is saved only on your own device using the app's local storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share a friend code yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Friend codes

If you tap "Share my code", the game creates a text code with the rider name you typed, your best scores and (if you choose) your custom stage, and hands it to your phone's share menu. You decide who to send it to. We never see it.

Permissions

The app only uses vibration, for rumble feedback while riding. It does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact email on the Powder Peak page in the Google Play Store.
